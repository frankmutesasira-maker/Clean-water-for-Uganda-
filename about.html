Our mission

Our mission is to help expand access to safe, reliable water through practical projects that respond to local needs. We focus on solutions such as boreholes, shallow wells and solar-powered water systems where these approaches are technically and locally appropriate.

We believe water projects should be designed around the people who will use them, with clear project goals, responsible implementation and honest reporting.

Who we serve

Children & vulnerable families

Supporting safer water access and healthier environments for children and families who face barriers to reliable water.

Schools

Helping schools explore practical water and hygiene infrastructure that supports learning and daily life.

Churches & community institutions

Working with trusted local institutions where a water project can serve a wider community.

Underserved communities

Prioritising communities identified through local engagement and needs verification rather than assuming that district-wide statistics describe every village.

How we approach projects

1. Listen and assess

We seek information from communities and local stakeholders about water access, existing sources, population needs and practical challenges.

2. Verify the need

We assess whether a proposed project is appropriate before presenting a specific location as a confirmed fundraising opportunity.

3. Plan the solution

Technical scope, site conditions, water-source feasibility, equipment and estimated costs should be considered before implementation.

4. Document progress

As evidence becomes available, we aim to publish appropriate project updates, water-testing information, photographs and completion evidence.

Where we are prioritising work

Current priority-screening areas include Hoima, Kiryandongo, Nakasongola, Nakaseke, Kyankwanzi and Luweero. These are priority areas for assessment and community engagement, not a claim that every community within them lacks safe water.

District-level information is used for screening. Specific project locations should be verified locally before fundraising or implementation.

Our commitment to transparency

We distinguish fundraising targets from verified results. We aim to report verified donation progress, appropriate project records, implementation evidence and relevant updates as they become available.

We also respect donor and beneficiary privacy. Personal information should not be published simply because someone supports a project, and beneficiary stories or images should be shared only when appropriate consent and safeguarding requirements are met.

Important information for donors

Clean Water for Uganda presents project targets and planned activities honestly. A fundraising target is not the same as money already received, and a proposed project is not presented as completed until there is appropriate evidence.
