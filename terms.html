Website Terms

This website provides information about Clean Water for Uganda, its projects, fundraising campaigns and partnership opportunities.

Fundraising information

Project targets are fundraising goals unless explicitly identified as verified funds received. Planned work, estimates and targets must not be interpreted as completed results.

Donation references

A donation reference records an intended contribution and does not itself constitute proof that funds have been received or verified.

Accuracy

We aim to keep published information accurate and update it as verified information becomes available.

Contact

Questions about projects, donations or published information should be directed through the Contact page.
