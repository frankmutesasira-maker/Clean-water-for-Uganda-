ACCOUNTABILITY • EVIDENCE • DONOR PRIVACY

Trust is built with evidence.

We want donors, partners and communities to understand what we are fundraising for, how projects are verified, and what information we publish. We will separate fundraising claims from verified project results and update this page as documented information becomes available.

Verified information only

Our transparency commitments

1. Clear project purpose

Each fundraising campaign should identify the project, intended location or community, target amount and the main activities the funds are expected to support.

2. Verification before reporting impact

We will not present targets, estimates or unverified reports as completed impact. Project results will be updated when supporting evidence is available.

3. Financial accountability

Donation records are intended to move through review before they are counted as verified campaign funds. Project spending should be supported by appropriate records such as receipts, invoices or payment evidence.

4. Evidence from the field

Where available and appropriate, we will publish project photos, water-testing information, implementation updates, completion evidence and other documentation connected to the project.

5. Donor privacy

Donors should never be required to publish their identity. If a donor chooses the public donation-wall option, only the information described on the donation form should be displayed.

6. Corrections

If published information is found to be inaccurate, outdated or incomplete, we will correct or update it rather than presenting an unsupported claim as fact.

How a donation is treated

Donation submitted

A donor provides the requested donation information and selects a payment method.

Pending review

The submission is treated as pending until the contribution can be matched and reviewed.

Finance verification

Payment evidence and relevant donation information are checked before the amount is included in verified campaign reporting.

Project reporting

Verified campaign information can be reflected in project progress. Supporting project evidence is added as implementation proceeds.

What we publish

  • Project purpose and fundraising target.
  • Verified fundraising progress when reliable records are available.
  • Project location and implementation updates when it is safe and appropriate to publish them.
  • Relevant water-testing and completion evidence when available.
  • Selected financial and project documentation that can be shared without exposing private donor or beneficiary information.

We will not publish donor addresses, private contact information, payment credentials or other sensitive personal information. Beneficiary information and images should only be shared where appropriate consent and safeguarding requirements have been met.

Questions about accountability?

Donors and partners can contact us with questions about a project, fundraising record or published information. We welcome reasonable requests for clarification and corrections.
