How projects move forward

  1. Community need is identified.
  2. Existing water access and local conditions are assessed.
  3. Technical feasibility and project scope are reviewed.
  4. Fundraising and implementation proceed when appropriate.
  5. Progress and evidence are documented as the project develops.

Project targets are fundraising goals. They should not be interpreted as amounts already raised or spent unless the site explicitly reports verified figures.
