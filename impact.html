How we define impact

  • Verified: supported by records available to the reporting system.
  • Ongoing: implementation is underway and evidence is still being collected.
  • Planned: proposed work that has not yet been completed.
  • Target: a fundraising or long-term goal, not a completed result.

Impact reporting

As projects progress, we aim to add appropriate information such as verified project status, communities reached, water-system completion evidence and relevant water-testing information. We will avoid publishing unsupported beneficiary totals.
