Privacy Policy

Clean Water for Uganda respects donor, partner and visitor privacy.

Information we collect

When you use the donation reference form, we may collect the information you submit, such as name, email, country, phone number, donation amount, frequency, selected project and payment method.

How information is used

Information is used to record, verify and administer donations, communicate about projects, respond to enquiries and maintain accountability.

Public donation wall

Public display is optional. If selected, the site may display only the information described on the donation form. Email addresses, phone numbers, payment credentials and other private information are not published.

Payment information

The website does not request passwords, PINs or card security codes. The donation page provides Western Union, MoneyGram and MTN Mobile Money instructions.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on our Contact page.
