WHY IT MATTERS

Clean water changes everything.

Reliable safe water supports health, education, dignity and stronger communities. Clean Water for Uganda focuses on practical projects that can be understood, funded and tracked.

Our approach

Community-led. We work toward solutions designed around local needs.

Sustainable. Water infrastructure should serve communities beyond the fundraising campaign.

Transparent. Verified donations, project progress and financial records are designed to connect through one system.
