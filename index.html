📍 Communities that urgently need clean water

Some Ugandan communities continue to face long distances to water, unsafe sources and seasonal shortages. We are building our project pipeline around verified community needs and local assessments.

Bulundira East

Community-reported water needs include lack of nearby borehole access. Project details should be independently verified before fundraising.

Ongojje West

Community reports describe households facing significant water-collection burdens and children missing school to help collect water.

Nabwanga

Community reports describe long journeys to collect water, including reports of households walking several kilometres for a jerrycan.

Bukewa East

Community reports describe reliance on ponds and seasonal water shortages during dry periods.

Source & verification

The community descriptions above are based on information published by Hope for Every Heart. We present them as source material, not as independently verified current assessments by Clean Water for Uganda. Before adopting a community as a fundraising project, we will verify the location, population, water sources, technical feasibility and current need.
