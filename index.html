How we work

From community need to documented project.

01 — LISTENUnderstand the local need.

Work with communities and institutions to identify the problem.

02 — VERIFYAssess the situation.

Review existing water sources, local conditions and technical feasibility.

03 — BUILDImplement appropriately.

Develop the project when funding, scope and feasibility align.

04 — REPORTPublish evidence.

Document progress, costs, testing and completion evidence.

Where we are focusing

Priority areas for assessment and engagement.

Our current priority districts include Hoima, Kiryandongo, Nakasongola, Nakaseke, Kyankwanzi and Luweero. District priority does not mean every community in the district lacks safe water; specific needs are verified before projects are presented as fundraising campaigns.

Hoima

Community needs assessment and project identification.

Kiryandongo

Identify specific underserved communities through local assessment.

Nakasongola

Assess remaining community water gaps and feasible solutions.

Nakaseke

Verify local needs before project selection.

Kyankwanzi

Priority area for further assessment and water infrastructure development.

Luweero

Prioritise communities with verified water-access gaps.
